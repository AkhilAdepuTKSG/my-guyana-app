Sample documents for scanning

Use these to try the “Scan a document to fill this in” feature in My Guyana. They’re fictional — invented names and numbers.

  1. Download a document below (or open this page on another screen).
  2. In My Guyana, open an application (e.g. Passport) or the e-ID / manual sign-up, and tap Scan a document.
  3. Choose the saved image from your photos, or photograph the document on the other screen.
  4. OCR runs on your device and fills in what it can — check every field.
