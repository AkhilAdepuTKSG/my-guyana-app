Sample documents & test identities

Two fictional citizens the demo “government database” already knows. Sign in or create an account with their numbers, or scan a card to try on-device OCR. Every document for a person is internally consistent — the same name, date of birth and numbers throughout.

  1. Pick a citizen below. To sign in, use Nicole’s e-ID number and date of birth. To create an account, use any of John’s document numbers.
  2. Or download a card and tap Scan a document in the app to fill fields by OCR.
  3. OCR runs on your device and fills in what it can — check every field.
